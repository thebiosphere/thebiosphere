OUR PURPOSE

Go beyond
the curriculum.

Science competitions give students an opportunity to explore biology beyond the limits of a regular classroom.

Through preparation and participation, members can strengthen their scientific knowledge while developing problem-solving, critical-thinking, and time-management skills.

Most importantly, competitions give us another way to challenge ourselves and learn together.
