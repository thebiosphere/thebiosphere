We believe that understanding biology also means taking action to protect and support the living world around us.

OUR PURPOSE

Biology
beyond the
classroom.

Volunteering gives students an opportunity to connect what they learn about biology with the world around them.

Through environmental and community activities, we hope to contribute to the protection of ecosystems while encouraging students to become more aware of the biological world.

Our volunteering activities will be developed around meaningful service, collaboration, and environmental awareness.
