ABOUT THE BIOSPHERE

Curiosity
becomes
discovery.

The Biosphere is an independent, student-led science club especially focused on biology.

Our club explores the field of biology through four main pillars: research, volunteering, competition, and science media.

Our main goal is to investigate biology more deeply while spreading knowledge about species, biological concepts, ecological conservation, and other scientific topics to students and the public.

OUR MISSION

02 / 04

Explore.
Investigate.
Share.

We believe that biology is more than a subject in a textbook.

It is a way to understand the organisms, ecosystems, and processes that shape the world around us.

Through our work, we want to create a community where students can learn, investigate, communicate, and make meaningful contributions.
