OUR APPROACH

Curiosity
comes first.

Biology is more than memorizing facts. It is about asking questions about the living world and finding ways to investigate those questions.

Through The Biosphere, students will have opportunities to explore biological topics, examine existing research, and develop their own investigations.

Our goal is to make research accessible to students while encouraging careful observation, critical thinking, and scientific communication.
